OV(T) — Full Curve

OV(T) OV = 0 T* (optimal)
▸ show decision rule

DISPOSE NOW  ⟺  OV(T*)≤0 (no year, not even T=0, ever pays off)
PRESERVE OPTION until T*  ⟺  T*>0 AND OV(T*)>0 (waiting is worth it)
ACT NOW  ⟺  T*=0 AND OV(T*)>0 (the payoff is real, best pursued immediately — not the same as disposal)

A positive T* alone was never sufficient on its own — it can be the least-negative point on an unprofitable curve, which is why OV(T*)>0 is required in every branch. This three-way version was added after a red-team test found that T*=0 with a positive OV(T*) was being mislabeled as "DISPOSE NOW," when it actually means the reprocessing pathway is worth pursuing immediately.

Parameter registry (provisional v0.3)

Origin, evidence quality and leverage of the 14 inputs
Origin and evidence quality are separate: origin says where a value comes from (sourced, scenario, illustrative, unknown), quality says how defensible it is as a real-world estimate. A star on sourced means the exact source is still to be verified. Leverage is computed from the model at the current settings: the nearest value of that single input at which the verdict changes. This is a provisional metadata audit, not validation of the model equations.
⚠ THIS MODEL DOES NOT PREDICT THE FUTURE It evaluates the economic consequences of explicitly stated assumptions and scenarios. Changing the assumptions changes the result. This formula has already been revised once, after we found and fixed an internal inconsistency (C_avoided was being credited independently of technology success) — it may be revised again. If you find something broken, that is the point of publishing it.
